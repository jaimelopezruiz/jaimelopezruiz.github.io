Reusable Rocket Body and Feed System
First student-built rocket to hot fire vertically in the UK.
Design, manufacture and testing of a reusable liquid bipropellant rocket body and feed system as part of a student rocket programme at Imperial College.

The team developed a compact piston-driven tank and piping system to store and deliver ethanol and nitrous oxide within an integrated, mass-efficient structure. Designed pressure-bearing components, sealing systems, and internal plumbing to ensure reliable propellant delivery.

The project involved intensive iterative design and preparation, in close integration with engine and control subteams. The system was validated through hydrostatic, cold-flow, and hot-fire testing (shown in video).

Computational
Physics-Informed Raman Fitting Pipeline for Irradiated SiC
Config-driven Python pipeline for quantitative Raman analysis of ion-irradiated SiC, carrying a propagated uncertainty on every reported number.

Per-sample YAML physics inputs, auto-detected, drive region-wise fitting of overlapping peaks with Gaussian, Lorentzian, Voigt and Breit–Wigner–Fano line shapes. Every peak parameter carries a 1σ uncertainty propagated from the fit covariance and validated against Monte Carlo to 0.1%, alongside automated fit-quality diagnostics and flags. RamanSPy is used for parts of the spectral handling; the fitting, uncertainty and metrics layers are the project's own.

Derived material metrics — chemical-disorder ratio, a coupling-invariant crystallinity index, and residual stress and strain mapping with a stated detection floor — come with explicit uncertainty budgets separating statistical from systematic error. Regression tests and scripted figures mean every quantitative result and figure in the manuscript regenerates from tracked code, configs and raw spectra.

Python Model of Lithium-Ion Transport in Electrolyte (Explicit & Implicit PDE Solvers)
Numerical modelling of lithium-ion transport in a battery electrolyte using finite difference methods to simulate diffusion-convection dynamics during discharge.

The project formulates and solves a 1D convection-diffusion PDE describing how lithium-ion concentration evolves across the electrolyte, using both explicit (FTCS) and implicit (Crank-Nicolson) schemes, then compares their stability and accuracy.
