03Projects

Mechanical Design
Reusable Rocket Body and Feed System
First student-built rocket to hot-fire vertically in the UK.
Design, manufacture and testing of a reusable liquid bipropellant rocket body and feed system as part of a student rocket programme at Imperial College.

The team developed a compact piston-driven tank and piping system to store and deliver ethanol and nitrous oxide within an integrated, mass-efficient structure. Designed pressure-bearing components, sealing systems, and internal plumbing to ensure reliable propellant delivery.

The project involved intensive iterative design and preparation, in close integration with engine and control subteams. The system was validated through hydrostatic, cold-flow, and hot-fire testing (shown in video).

Robot Arm Markerless Teleoperation
Real-time teleoperation of the SO-101 5-DOF robot arm from a standard webcam — no markers, no wearables.

The user's arm motion is captured by a webcam, processed via pose estimation, and translated into joint commands that drive the physical robot in real time. No wearable trackers, no IMUs, no fiducial markers.

Computational
Lithium-Ion Transport in a Battery Electrolyte
Numerical modelling of lithium-ion transport during discharge, using finite difference methods to simulate diffusion–convection dynamics in Python.

Formulates and solves a 1D convection–diffusion PDE describing how lithium-ion concentration evolves across the electrolyte, using both explicit (FTCS) and implicit (Crank–Nicolson) schemes.
