02Thesis

A demonstration is useful for what it does to the object. My hand touches the box wherever it likes, and the SO-101 has a single small pusher tip and a different reach, so my hand motion would not transfer to it. The path of the box does.

The residual policy matches the scripted pusher and finishes about 25% sooner. On the four held-out letters it tracks closer (0.51 against 0.65 cm). Plain PPO, without the scripted pusher underneath, never learned the task.

05What didn’t work

The first residual reward paid about 20 times more for finishing sooner than the extra deviation cost, so the policy got faster and less accurate. Weighting the penalty by box displacement took it from 6/7 at 0.88 cm to 7/7 at 0.63 cm.

8 of 200 “converged” IK answers were off by up to 4.9 mm, because joints were clamped after the convergence check. Every IK solution is now re-checked with forward kinematics.
