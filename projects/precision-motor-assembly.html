Precision motor
assembly

My main project during my 12-month placement at PerkinElmer: a coupled, synchronised precision motor assembly with a pre-loaded friction-drive reduction, reaching 0.001° angular resolution in the optical path of a spectroscopy instrument.

Employer
PerkinElmer
Role
Mechanical Design Engineer
Resolution
0.001°
Home-return accuracy
65″ → 6″
AThe drive

I designed the coupled, synchronised motor assembly with a pre-loaded friction-drive reduction, and built the metrology to prove it, qualifying the axis against an autocollimator reference.

BError budget

I characterised and decomposed the drive’s angular error budget: servo following error, friction hysteresis, encoder quantisation and thermal mount drift. Re-tuning the position loop for the loaded axis improved home-return accuracy ten-fold, from 65″ to 6″.

CRepeatability

Friction hysteresis below one encoder count showed up as travel-dependent micro-slip that the closed loop could not see. I designed a deterministic approach strategy that restored repeatability, and found the structural resonance that limits servo damping on the compliant axis.

DOwnership

I owned the motion-control subsystem end to end: the Python/I2C driver and characterisation suite, step-scan synchronisation with the optical chopper with zero cumulative drift on the wavelength axis, and the drive’s optical acceptance criteria, defined with the optics engineers.

EProduction

Custom test jigs and assembly accessories cut production test time by over 4 hours (30%) per instrument. Across the components I owned: rapid prototyping (3D printing, machined parts), procurement, GD&T, FMEA and poka-yoke.

FWhy no pictures

The instrument is a commercial product and its design is PerkinElmer’s intellectual property, so I can’t show photographs, drawings or more detail than this sheet.
