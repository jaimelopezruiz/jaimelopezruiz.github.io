Section B-B · My scope

A compact, piston-driven tank and piping system stores and delivers ethanol and nitrous oxide inside an integrated, mass-efficient structure. I designed the pressure-bearing components, the sealing and the internal plumbing, working closely with the engine and control subteams.

The system was validated through hydrostatic, cold-flow and hot-fire campaigns.
