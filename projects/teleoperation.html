Markerless
teleoperation

An SO-101 arm driven from a single webcam. Pose estimation, a radial mapping from my arm to the robot's reach, and damped least-squares IK turn my movement into joint commands. No wearables, no markers.

04What I learned
  1. Determine joint signs in isolation. Teleop confounds too many errors at once to read a single sign off it.
  2. Mapping hand position straight to tool position made deep folds impossible to command, so I switched to the radial map.
  3. The arm jerked at startup for two separate reasons. A measured limp pose fixes the joint-space jerk, and a ready-pose gate fixes the task-space one.
