Beverage
chiller

A bottle that chills its drink and pumps it out, for hot climates. A week-long design competition; I led a team of four from concept to proposal.

Poster ↗
Format
Design Week, one week
Team
Four; I led
Bottle
750 ml
Production run
About 10,000

Section B-B · Cooling

A helical rod runs down the bottle: an insulating outer layer, propylene glycol gel, and a stainless inner wall against the drink. Rods swap, so a frozen one replaces a warm one.

Section C-C · Pump

A micro-diaphragm pump: an eccentric wheel flexes a diaphragm between two one-way valves. It copes with juices of different consistency and gives control over the flow. 3D-printed case, injection-moulded diaphragm.
