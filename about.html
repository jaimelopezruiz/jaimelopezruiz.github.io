About

Mechanical engineer. I build precise hardware and the software that drives it.

My work spans robotics and mechatronics, nuclear materials research and computational modelling. I am in the final year of an MEng at Imperial College London, doing my final-year project at the Hamlyn Centre for Robotics on autonomous cochlear implant insertion.

Before that I spent a year at PerkinElmer designing a precision motor assembly for a spectroscopy instrument, characterised irradiated silicon carbide at Oxford, built a markerless teleoperation pipeline for a robot arm, and led the feed system of a liquid rocket that hot fired.

Available from July 2027.
