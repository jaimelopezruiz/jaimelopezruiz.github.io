1Abstract

I investigated irradiation-induced damage in silicon carbide under nuclear reactor conditions using Raman spectroscopy, extending to in-situ high-temperature annealing and residual stresses in SiC/SiC composites, and built the analysis pipeline behind every quantitative result.

2How it started

Before Oxford, my literature research project at Imperial reviewed 65 sources on fusion breeder blankets, supervised by Dr Matt Eaton. It identified irradiation damage in SiC flow-channel inserts as a principal open gap for high-temperature dual-coolant lithium-lead designs, where SiC insulation enables roughly 45% thermodynamic efficiency against 34% for the low-temperature variant. That gap led to the Oxford work on ion-irradiated SiC. Read the review ↗

3Experiment

Single-crystal and reaction-bonded polycrystalline 6H-SiC across nine irradiated conditions: Ne and Si self-ions at three energies each (350 to 2000 keV), designed against SRIM to flatten the damage profile across the 1 µm Raman interaction depth, and Au at 3 MeV, spanning 0.25 to 8 dpa and room temperature to 750 °C, implanted at the Surrey Ion Beam Centre. Si-wafer and Li-implanted diamond specimens served as monatomic controls.

I ran the polycrystalline Raman campaign on a Renishaw inVia confocal microscope at 532 nm, with point-by-point stress maps across SiC grains and the residual silicon matrix, and in-situ annealing to 1200 °C on a Linkam TS1500 stage, with 10 °C steps around 800 °C to resolve the recovery onset.

4Pipeline

A Python pipeline for quantitative Raman analysis of irradiated ceramics: baseline correction, config-driven region-wise fitting of overlapping peaks (Gaussian, Lorentzian, Voigt, Breit–Wigner–Fano), 1σ uncertainties propagated from the fit covariance and validated against Monte Carlo to 0.1%, and automated fit-quality diagnostics.

Derived metrics (a chemical-disorder ratio, a coupling-invariant crystallinity index, and residual stress with a stated detection floor) carry uncertainty budgets that separate statistical from systematic error. Every reported value and figure regenerates from tracked code, configs and raw spectra. Code ↗
