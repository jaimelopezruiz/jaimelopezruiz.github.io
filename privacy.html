Sheet 05.1 · Privacy and credits

Privacy

This is my personal portfolio. It has no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no forms and no accounts, and it does not track you.

1Who runs it

Jaime López Ruiz. For anything on this page, email jaime@lopez-ruiz.com.

2What the site loads

Every page, script, font, image and video is served from this site. Nothing is requested from Google or any other third party when you open a page, and no third-party code runs here.

3Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement ↗. I do not receive or use those logs.

4One setting in your browser

If you use the Processed / Raw video switch, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage under the key jlr-raw, so it stays the same from page to page. Nothing is saved until you use the switch, and it never leaves your browser. To remove it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

5Email and links

If you email me, I use your message only to reply to you. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Hugging Face and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Content on this site is © 2026 Jaime López Ruiz unless noted; code in my linked repositories is under the licence stated in each repository. Rocket footage is from the Design Make & Test project with Imperial College Rocketry Society.

Fonts: Big Shoulders Display, and IBM Plex Mono, Sans and Serif by IBM, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the licences are served with the fonts.
