Sheet 01.1 · Privacy and credits

Privacy

This is my personal portfolio. It has no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no forms and no accounts, and it does not track you.

1Who runs it

Jaime López Ruiz. For anything on this page, email jaime@lopez-ruiz.com.

2What the site loads

Every page, script, font, image and video is served from this site. Nothing is requested from Google or any other third party when you open a page, and no third-party code runs here.

3Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement ↗. I do not receive or use those logs.

4Browser storage

The site does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser.

5Email and links

If you email me, I use your message only to reply to you. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Hugging Face and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.
